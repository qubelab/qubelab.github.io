01 / THE STORY✧
> NEW QUEST AVAILABLE

A small spin.
A big question.

Welcome to QUBE Lab at POSTECH, South Korea. We're experimental and theoretical biophysicists discovering how quantum spin effects shape biological functions—and how they could inspire new medical technologies.

We build optical instruments, develop new methods for spin-dynamics simulations, investigate protein motion using molecular dynamics, and explore quantum sensing in living cells and subcellular environments.
