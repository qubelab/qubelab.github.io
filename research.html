Five interconnected research areas

We combine optical instrumentation, spin chemistry experiments, quantum spin simulation, protein molecular dynamics, and cellular sensing.

01 / INSTRUMENTATION

Design & Build Optical Instruments

We design and construct optical instruments for investigating quantum spin effects. Our emphasis is on developing the experimental capabilities needed to generate, control and measure spin-dependent optical signals.

CUSTOM OPTICS · OPTICAL READOUTS · SPIN-DEPENDENT SIGNALS

Software & Methods for Spin Dynamics

03 / MOLECULAR MODELING

Molecular Dynamics of Proteins

We use molecular dynamics simulations to explore protein motions, conformational changes and local molecular environments. This molecular-scale perspective helps us study how structural dynamics may influence spin-active systems.

04 / BIOLOGICAL SENSING

Quantum Sensing in Cells & Subcellular Environments

We investigate the potential of quantum spin effects as sensing mechanisms in living cells and within subcellular environments, aiming to connect molecular-scale quantum physics with biologically relevant measurements.

05 / EXPERIMENTAL SPIN CHEMISTRY

Radical-Pair Photochemistry & Magnetosensitivity

We experimentally investigate spin-dependent photochemistry and magnetic-field sensitivity in flavins, membrane-associated radical pairs, and cryptochrome proteins. Spectroscopy, photochemical kinetics and magnetic-field-effect measurements connect molecular spin dynamics with measurable reaction outcomes.

FLAVIN PHOTOCHEMISTRY · RADICAL PAIRS · MAGNETIC FIELD EFFECTS
