MODEL / APPROXIMATIONS

One-dimensional B(x)=B0+Gx x and two-dimensional B(x,y)=B0+Gx x+Gy y. Exact two-electron singlet-product yields (equal-rate Haberkorn) with illustrative effective hyperfine fields. Optical proxy I/Icenter=1+α[ΦS(B)−ΦS(Bcenter)] uses a user-selected α, which is NOT measured optical calibration. No microscopy point-spread function, diffusion, photon noise, nonlinear saturation, or inhomogeneous excitation is represented.

All numerical parameters are generic educational inputs, not experimentally fitted molecular constants. Verify quantitative comparisons against validated spin-dynamics packages and experiment.
