MODEL / ASSUMPTIONS

Two electron spins (½ each) and one optional explicit nucleus: H/h = (μB/h)B(gA S1z + gB S2z) − (γn/2π)B Iz + Ssite·A·I + J S1·S2 + d[S1·S2 − 3(S1·r̂)(S2·r̂)]. Frequencies in MHz, B in mT, t in μs, γn/2π in MHz/T; A is axial along laboratory z. d = 52.041 MHz nm³ × (gA gB / 2.00231930436²)/r³. An initially unpolarized nucleus is combined with a selected electronic state. Equal Haberkorn rates kS = kT = k (μs⁻¹) are used: ρ(t) = exp(−kt) Uρ(0)U†; ΦS,T = k∫Tr(PS,T ρ)dt. Infinite-time integrals are evaluated analytically in the energy eigenbasis. No phenomenological spin relaxation or unequal-rate kinetics. All presets are illustrative, not fits to actual proteins.

Instantaneous selective rotations are ideal laboratory-frame operations at the specified delays, not frequency-selective finite pulses or a rotating-wave continuous-drive model. Products generated before a pulse are retained.
