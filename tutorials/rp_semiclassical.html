MODEL / APPROXIMATIONS

N independent nuclei with isotropic contact coupling A_i. Each contributes a normally distributed three-vector with component standard deviation A_i sqrt(I_i(I_i+1)/3). Electron spins evolve exactly under those sampled static Overhauser fields (bath quantum backaction, nuclear quadrupolar couplings and time dependence omitted). Ensemble yield is averaged with a fixed seed. CPU is limited to 128 samples and 12 nuclei.

All numerical parameters are generic educational inputs, not experimentally fitted molecular constants. Verify quantitative comparisons against validated spin-dynamics packages and experiment.
