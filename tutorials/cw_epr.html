MODEL / ASSUMPTIONS

H/h = (μB/h) B·g·S + D[Sz² − S(S+1)/3]. Exact electron-only eigenenergies and transverse transition strengths are calculated at each field and sampled orientation. Hyperfine is first-order with one nucleus and axial collinear tensors. Equal population-difference weighting (high-temperature approximation), 39 orientation samples, and a three-point Gaussian D-strain quadrature (nodes 0 and ±√3σ; weights 2/3 and 1/6 each) are used. Lorentzian broadening is specified as field FWHM; no nuclear quadrupole, exchange, or fitting. Presets are representative illustrative values, not fitted species-specific parameters.

Educational implementation; validate against a dedicated spin dynamics package before using results for quantitative experimental analysis.
