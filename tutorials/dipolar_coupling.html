MODEL / ASSUMPTIONS

d = 52.041 MHz nm³ · (g₁g₂/ge²)/r³. Full Hamiltonian H/h = (μB/h)B(g₁S₁z + g₂S₂z) + d[S₁·S₂ − 3(S₁·n)(S₂·n)]. The exact transition-spectrum panel diagonalizes this full 4×4 matrix. The DEER trace and Pake panel deliberately use the ideal high-field secular modulation frequency d(1−3cos²θ), not exact pulse propagation at low field. Powder averaging uses uniformly spaced cosθ and Gaussian distance weights; no background decay or pulse bandwidth.

Educational implementation; validate against a dedicated spin dynamics package before using results for quantitative experimental analysis.
